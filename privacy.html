Jake Voice Assistant - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

About Jake

Jake is a privately operated voice assistant designed to help an authorized user interact with information and email through spoken commands.

Information Accessed

With the user's authorization, Jake accesses Gmail messages and associated information, including senders, recipients, subjects, and message contents.

Jake also processes spoken requests and conversation content to provide voice assistant functionality.

How Information Is Used

Gmail information is used to read messages, prepare replies, and compose or send emails requested by the user.

Outgoing emails require explicit spoken confirmation before they are sent.

Third-Party Services

Jake uses Google's Gmail API for authorized email functions and OpenAI services for voice interaction and language processing. Information necessary to fulfill a request may be processed by these services.

Data Storage and Security

Google authorization credentials are stored locally on the device. Access to the device and its credentials is restricted.

Jake is not intended to sell personal information or use Gmail information for advertising.

Data Retention and Deletion

Authorization credentials remain on the device until removed or revoked. Temporary email drafts and confirmation data are held in memory during operation and are cleared when canceled, expired, or when the application exits.

The account owner may revoke Jake's Google access through their Google Account security settings. Locally stored credentials may also be deleted by the device administrator.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the developer through the support email listed on Jake's Google OAuth consent screen.
